農林水産省選定の牛舎見守りAI「農家のミハルAI」、全国で無料相談を開始

"Noka no Miharu AI," a barn-monitoring AI for dairy and Wagyu farmers, has started offering free nationwide consultation after being selected by Japan's Ministry of Agriculture, Forestry and Fisheries. It is drawing attention as a practical AI deployment case in agriculture, aimed at easing labor shortages and inspection workloads.
